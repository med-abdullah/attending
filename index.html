Install

Two minutes, once.

Download it on your Mac, not your phone. Updates after this are automatic.

Download and open the DMG

Click Download for Mac, then open the file from Downloads.

Drag Attending to Applications

Drop the icon onto the Applications folder in the window that opens.

Open it, then click Done

macOS says it can't verify the developer the first time. Click Done, not Move to Trash.

Allow it in System Settings

Apple menu › System Settings › Privacy & Security. Scroll to Security and click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.

You only do the last two steps once; Attending isn't notarized by Apple yet. If Open Anyway isn't there, open Attending again and go straight back to Privacy & Security. Attending then welcomes you and walks through each permission, all optional.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Attending is free and open source under the MIT license. If it helps you, you can buy me a coffee.

What does it collect?

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics and no servers. Your calendar, study data and settings stay on your Mac. Window titles are read in the moment, and only totals are kept.

Do I need a MacBook with a notch?

No. It's best with a notch, but it also sits below the menu bar on other Macs and external displays, and you can drag it anywhere.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 14.2 Sonoma or later. Intel Macs aren't supported.

Does it work with Anki, UWorld and AMBOSS?

Anki works through the free AnkiConnect add-on. Log your UWorld or AMBOSS questions for a daily pace today; a question block timer that follows those pages is coming soon. Attending isn't affiliated with any of them.

Something isn't working

Open Settings › Help › Report a problem in the app, or open an issue on GitHub.
